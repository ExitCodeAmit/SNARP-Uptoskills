## Roles and words
- Member: read, post, comment, follow, message, join communities, register for events, make stories and reels. Cannot submit papers, peer-review or create events.
- Author: everything a Member can do, plus submit papers, peer-review other people's papers, and create events.
- Moderator: given only by the Super Admin. Peer-reviews, verifies papers in the Moderator dashboard (approve, reject, request changes), can remove posts, comments and reels, manage communities and events. Moderators cannot submit papers.
- Super Admin: runs the platform. Shown as "Author" on the main site.
- Members and Authors can switch between those two roles themselves in [Settings](/settings?tab=account) under Account type.
- Friends = people you follow who also follow you back. Story mentions, the Friends story audience and paper co-authors are limited to friends.
- Blocking: only the Super Admin can block (suspend) accounts. Members, Authors and Moderators cannot block or mute other people. To flag a harmful post use **Report** on that post (reports exist only for feed posts, not profiles or messages); for anything else contact support.
- Doing too many actions quickly (about 30 per minute) shows "You are doing that too often…"; wait a minute.

## Sign up, log in and passwords
- Sign up at [Create an account](/signup): Full name (2–100 characters), Email, Password, Confirm password. Under "I want to" choose **Read & discuss** (Member) or **Publish papers** (Author), then **Create account**.
- Password: 8+ characters with letters and a number or symbol, not a common password, not containing your name or email. A strength meter helps.
- After signing up, confirm your email from the link we send ("Confirm your SNARP email address"). The link works for 24 hours. **Resend verification email** can be used once a minute. You cannot log in before confirming.
- Log in at [Log in](/login). **Keep me logged in for 7 days** is ticked by default; untick it on shared computers (then the session ends when the browser closes).
- Forgot password: on the login page choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email, **Send link**. The link lasts 60 minutes. Set the new password and you are signed out everywhere, then log in again.
- Suspended accounts see "This account has been suspended". Contact support.

## Layout
- Left sidebar (on phones open it with the ☰ button): your profile card with Posts, **Followers** and **Following** (these open a list where you can **Follow back** or unfollow), then the menu: [Feed](/), [Reels](/reels), [Papers](/articles), [Docs](/docs), [Messages](/messages), [Communities](/communities), [Peer review](/reviews) for Authors, Moderators and Super Admin, **Review queue** for Moderators, and [Settings](/settings). **Sign out** is at the bottom.
- Top bar: **Search** (suggestions after 2 letters: topics, papers, researchers), the **bell** for [Notifications](/notifications), and **Submit paper**.
- The round sparkle button at the bottom right opens SNARP Guide (this assistant). The [Help page](/help) has the FAQ and contact details.

## Feed
- The [Feed](/) starts with Stories, then the post box "What are you working on…?".
- Posting: write up to 5,000 characters, add one image with **Photo** (or paste / drag it in): JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP up to 5 MB. Press **Post** (or Ctrl+Enter).
- Filters: **All / Posts / Papers** and **Everyone / Following** (Following = people you follow plus you). Scroll for more; a "new updates" pill appears when new items arrive.
- On a post: **Like**, **Comments** (opens a comment sheet, up to 2,000 characters per comment), **Share** (share sheet or copy link), **Report** (other people's posts; give a reason of 5–1,000 characters, once per post) and the views count on the right.
- Your own post: pencil to **Edit** the text (the image cannot be changed), trash to **Delete**. Moderators see **Remove post**.
- You can delete your own comments; the post author and moderators can remove any comment on it.
- Views = how many different people have seen a post or paper (counted when at least half the card is on screen for a second, or when the paper page is opened). Your own views are not counted. Older items started counting recently, so they may show few views.
- Paper cards in the feed show the paper, a PDF row with **Read PDF** and **Download**, and Like, Comments, Share, **Save**. The **⋯** button at the top right has **View details** and **Copy link**.
- Right side: **Requests** (people who follow you that you don't follow back: **Accept** follows them back, **Decline** hides them on this device) and **Suggestion for you** (people with shared research interests).

## Stories
- Make one: press **Add Story** or the **+** on your picture in the Stories row.
- Text: tap the story ("Tap to type") or press **Aa**. With text selected: size slider on the left, 9 colours at the bottom, **A** for a coloured box behind the text, trash, **Done**. Drag text to move it, drag it onto the bin to delete it. Each text up to 150 characters.
- Text-only stories: the round button at the top switches between 6 gradient backgrounds.
- Mention friends with **@** (friends only, up to 10); they get a notification. Up to 12 texts and mentions per story.
- Photo or video: **Photo / video** button. Photos JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP (large photos are shrunk automatically). Videos MP4, MOV or WebM up to 40 MB and 60 seconds; videos longer than 15 seconds are split automatically into parts of up to 15 seconds, each its own story.
- Who can see it, when sharing: the story editor has two share buttons at the bottom right. Press **Your story** to share with all followers, or the green **Friends** button to share only with followers you follow back. There is no separate pop-up. Stories last 24 hours.
- Who can see it, after sharing: open your own story (tap your picture in the Stories row), press the **N views · Followers/Friends** button at the bottom, and choose **Followers** or **Friends** under **Who can see this story**. You can have up to 10 active stories (each video part counts).
- Watching: tap the sides or use arrows to move, press and hold to pause, mute button for videos. Seen stories show a dimmed ring.
- On your own story: the **N views · Followers/Friends** button at the bottom opens who viewed it and **Who can see this story**, where you can switch between Followers and Friends at any time (for split videos it changes all parts). The trash button deletes the story (for split videos, only the current part).

## Reels
- Open [Reels](/reels). Tabs: **For you**, [Saved](/reels?tab=saved), [Your reels](/reels?tab=mine).
- Watching: scroll one reel at a time; they autoplay muted (sound button at the top right). Tap to pause, double-tap to like. Keyboard: ↑/↓ or j/k to move, m to mute.
- Right-side buttons: **Like**, **Comments**, **Share** (send to people in Messages or **Copy link**), **Save**, and **⋯**.
- **⋯** on your own reel lists: **Edit** (only caption, location and alt text), **Hide like count to others**, **Turn off commenting**, **Copy link**, **Delete**. Moderators can **Remove reel**.
- To turn off comments or hide likes after sharing: press **⋯** on the reel and choose **Turn off commenting** or **Hide like count to others** directly in that menu (they are not inside Edit). Press it again to turn it back on.
- If an author hides counts, others see "Likes" instead of a number. If commenting is off, others cannot comment. Only the owner sees the views count.
- Make a reel: **New reel** at the top right of Reels opens the studio:
  1. **Select**: drag a video in or **Select from computer** (MP4, WebM or MOV, up to 100 MB; the video can be up to 15 minutes, the reel plays up to 3 minutes). Uploading starts right away.
  2. **Crop**: bottom-left buttons choose the shape (Original, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9) and zoom; drag the picture to reposition it. **Next**.
  3. **Edit**: the **Filters** tab has Original, Bright, Warm, Cool, Mono, Vintage, Fade, Vivid, Dusk. The **Edit** tab has **Cover photo** (slide to pick a frame or **Select from computer** for a photo), **Trim** (drag the two handles; 1 second to 3 minutes) and the **Video sound** switch. **Next**.
  4. **Share** step: caption up to 2,200 characters with an emoji button, **Add location**, **Tag people** (up to 20, they are notified), **Accessibility** (alt text), **Advanced settings** (**Hide like and view counts on this reel**, **Turn off commenting**). Press **Share**, wait for "Your reel has been shared.", then **Done**. The new reel appears at the top of [Reels](/reels) and under the **Your reels** tab (reels are not shown on profile pages).
- Going back from Crop or closing the studio asks "Discard reel?".

## Papers: reading
- [Papers](/articles) has tabs **All papers**, [Saved](/saved) and [My papers](/my-submissions).
- Search by title, abstract, DOI or keyword, filter by **Author**, **Tag**, **Category** and **Sort**, then **Search**. 12 per page.
- "Recommended for you" suggests papers from your likes, saves, follows and research interests (add interests in [Settings](/settings)).
- A paper page shows authors, peer-review badge and average stars, DOI, abstract, keywords, the full paper (**Read PDF** opens a reader over the page; **Download** too), Like, Comments, Share, **Save**, and related papers.
- Only published papers are visible to everyone.
- Citations: open [Communities](/communities), tab **Citations** (or [this page](/tools)), fill in Authors, Title, Journal, Year, Volume, Issue, Pages, DOI and copy the **APA** or **BibTeX** result.

## Submitting a paper
- Who: Authors (and the Super Admin). Members see **Switch to Author** on the submit page or can change Account type in [Settings](/settings?tab=account). Moderators cannot submit.
- Start with **Submit paper** in the top bar ([Submit paper](/submit)):
  1. Drag the PDF onto the upload box or click it to choose the file (PDF only, 10 MB max). **Suggest details** reads it and fills title, abstract, domain, DOI, keywords, co-authors who are your friends, and notes (marked "From PDF"; **Undo all suggestions** to clear). Or **Skip smart fill**.
  2. Paper details: **Paper title** (required, up to 300 characters), **Abstract** (required), **Research domain** (required), DOI (optional), Keywords (up to 20, comma separated).
  3. Authors & submit: you are the first author. **Add a co-author** lists your friends only (up to 25 authors), each with an optional affiliation. Optional **Notes for reviewers**. Press **Submit for review**.
- Statuses in [My papers](/my-submissions): **Co-author check** (co-authors must approve the PDF) → **Waiting for moderator** → **Under review** → **Changes requested** or **Approved** or **Rejected** (final) → **Published** (only after the main author publishes).
- Co-authors: open the paper from the notification or My papers, check the PDF, choose **Approve this version** or **Request changes** (with a comment), **Save my check**. When everyone approves, the paper goes to review. If changes are requested, the submitter uses **Upload a corrected PDF** and **Send to co-authors**.
- Reviews are shown anonymously ("Reviewer 1"…) on the submission page, with versions and a timeline. Reviews for a round become visible after the moderator decides.
- Changes requested: mark each reviewer suggestion **Addressed** or **Declined** (with a reason), write a **Response to reviewers** (20+ characters), optionally upload a revised PDF and edit details, then **Resubmit for round N**.
- Approved: only the main submitter can publish, with **Publish for everyone** on the submission page or **Publish** in Peer review. Publishing cannot be undone.
- **Withdraw** (owner, any status except Published) permanently deletes the submission.

## Peer review
- For Authors, Moderators and the Super Admin: [Peer review](/reviews). You cannot review your own or co-authored papers, and reviewers stay anonymous to authors.
- Tabs **Needs review** and **My reviews**. "Your papers in progress" shows co-author approvals, review counts and **Publish** when approved.
- Review form: **Overall rating** (1–5 stars), scores 1–5 for **Originality, Methodology, Clarity, Significance**, **Recommendation** (Accept, Minor revision, Major revision, Reject), **Comments for the author** (20+ characters), **Suggestions for improvement** (needed when recommending a revision), optional **Note for moderators** (never shown to the author). **Submit review**; you can update it while the paper is still in review.
- Moderators then verify and decide. Approval does not publish; the author publishes.

## Communities and events
- [Communities](/communities): search, **Join** / **Leave**, **Share**. Anyone can create one with **New community** (name, research topic, description) and becomes its Owner.
- In a community, members post discussions (up to 5,000 characters). Owners and community moderators can remove posts, **Edit** the community and **Make mod** / **Remove mod**. The Owner cannot leave but can **Disband** (deletes it for everyone).
- [Events](/events): search, **Register** / **Cancel registration** (until the event starts; **Full** when capacity is reached). Authors, Moderators and the Super Admin can create one with **New event** (title, type, description, location or link, start and end, optional capacity) and **Publish event**. Organisers can **Edit event** or **Cancel event**.

## Docs (collaborative documents)
- [Docs](/docs) in the sidebar is a shared writing space for research documents (papers, literature reviews, lab notes). Anyone can use it.
- Create: press **New document**, give a title (and optional description), **Create document**. It opens straight in the editor.
- Writing: type on the page. The toolbar has **Undo/Redo**, normal text, **Heading 1/2**, font and size (8–72 pt), **Bold**, **Italic**, **Strikethrough**, inline code, alignment (left, center, right, justify), bulleted and numbered lists, quote and divider line. Everything saves automatically; the status under the title shows "Saving…" and then "Saved" with the time.
- Working together: everyone with access can be in the document at once. You see each person's coloured cursor with their name, and their picture at the top right (green dot = online, coloured ring = working now). Hovering text shows "Added by" and the writer's name.
- Share (owner and anyone with Can edit): press **Share**. Your friends (people you follow who follow you back) are listed straight away; press **Invite** next to one, or search anyone on SNARP by name. Choose **Can edit** or **Can view** first. They get a notification and an invitation at the top of Docs and must **Accept** before they can open it (or **Decline**). Only the owner can change someone's access or remove them; the owner can **Cancel** any waiting invitation and editors can cancel the ones they sent. View-only people see a **People** button listing who has access.
- View-only people can read but not type; to edit, ask the owner for Can edit.
- The **⋯** menu next to Share: **Rename / describe** (owner), **Save as PDF** (opens the print window; choose "Save as PDF" as the printer), **Delete document** (owner, deletes it for everyone) or **Leave document** (others).
- On the Docs page: filter **All / Owned by me / Shared with me**, search titles and descriptions, and use each card's **⋯** to open, rename, delete or leave.

## Messages
- Start a chat: open [Messages](/messages), type a name (or institution, department, interest) in the search box labelled "New message: search people" above your conversations, then click the person. There is no separate New message button. Or press **Message** on someone's profile. No follow needed.
- Type in the box at the bottom of the chat and press the paper-plane send button.
- Text messages up to 5,000 characters. Unread counts show on each chat and on the sidebar.
- Reels can be sent to people from the reel's **Share** button.

## Notifications
- The bell in the top bar opens [Notifications](/notifications): likes and comments on your posts, papers and reels, new followers, messages, story mentions, reel tags, paper review updates, review requests and co-author updates.
- Clicking one opens the right place and marks it read. **Mark all as read** clears them all.

## Search and profiles
- The top search shows researchers, posts, topics, papers, communities and events ([Search](/search)).
- A profile shows name, role, institution, bio, interests, skills, links, Posts, Papers, Followers and Following. Others' profiles have **Follow** and **Message**; yours has **Edit profile**. Tabs **Posts** and **Papers**.

## Settings
- [Settings](/settings), tab **Profile**: photo (JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP, 5 MB), full name, affiliation, department, bio, research interests (up to 20), skills (up to 30), professional links (up to 5, https only). **Save profile**. Email cannot be changed.
- Tab **Appearance**: theme **System / Light / Dark** and a dark mode switch.
- Tab **Account**: Account type (Member or Author; existing papers are kept), and **Delete account…** (needs your password; deletes your profile, posts, comments, messages, papers, and communities and events you organise; cannot be undone).
- Tab **Security**: to change your password fill **Current password**, **New password** and **Confirm**, then press the **Change password** button (this signs out your other devices; you cannot reuse the current password) and **Where you're logged in**: press **Sign out** next to one device, or **Sign out of N other devices** for all of them.
