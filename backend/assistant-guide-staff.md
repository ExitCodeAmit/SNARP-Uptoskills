## Moderator dashboard (staff only)
- Moderators open it from **Review queue** in the sidebar ([Moderator dashboard](/moderator)); the Super Admin can open it too.
- Sections: **Review queue**, **My reviews** (assigned to you), **Reviewed by me**, **My activity**, with counts of waiting, under review, assigned, approved-not-published and your decisions. Search by title, author, domain or tag.
- A paper shows the abstract, PDF, peer-review summary (including confidential reviewer notes), history and verification panel.
- Actions: **Start review** (claims it), **Approve** (optional message), **Request changes** (10–5,000 characters), **Reject** (reason; final), **Return to queue**. **Save note** adds an internal note only staff see.
- To request changes as a moderator: open the paper from **Review queue**, press **Start review** if nobody has claimed it, read the peer reviews, then press **Request changes**, write what the author must change (10–5,000 characters) and confirm. The paper becomes "Changes requested" and the author is notified. **Approve** and **Reject** work the same way.
- You cannot moderate your own or co-authored papers. A paper claimed by another moderator is locked (the Super Admin can override). Approval does not publish; the author publishes.

## Super Admin console (Super Admin only)
- A separate desk with its own sign-in for the Super Admin account only; the session ends when the tab closes. It has **Open site** and **Sign out**.
- **Overview**: statistics, papers by status, 14-day charts, recent activity, pending reports.
- **Users**: search and filter, user details, **Block/Unblock** (with a reason), **Make moderator** (confirmed, not blocked accounts), **Remove moderator**.
- **Moderators**: **Add a moderator** by email of an existing confirmed account, stats, **Suspend/Reactivate**, **Remove**.
- **Research papers**: filter by status and act like a moderator; **Change status** needs a reason.
- **Audit log**: every staff action, filterable and searchable.
- **Reported posts**: **Resolve** or **Dismiss** reports.
